Output File - Packet Switching vs Circuit Switching
===================================================


Simulation Results:

Packet Switching:
-----------------
- Packets from different sources were successfully transmitted simultaneously.
- Efficient sharing of bandwidth observed.
- Packets traveled independently through the router.
- Low delay and good resource utilization.

Circuit Switching Simulation:
-----------------------------
- Continuous ping created a dedicated-like path.
- When continuous traffic was running, other communications experienced higher delay.
- Resources remain reserved even during idle periods.
- Less flexible than packet switching.

Performance Summary:
- Packet Switching: Higher efficiency, better for data networks.
- Circuit Switching: Guaranteed path but poor bandwidth utilization.

Conclusion:
Packet Switching is far more suitable for modern computer networks due to its flexibility and efficient use of network resources.
